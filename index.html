Order Acceptance: Sellers receive every order as a pending request. The seller can accept or reject the request. If an order is rejected, the seller must provide a reason.

Menu Editing: Before an order is accepted, the seller can modify the menu details. Once an order has been accepted, the seller can only increase the available quantity and extend the meal availability time.

Cancellations: Free within 10 minutes of ordering. 50% refund if cancelled 10–30 minutes after ordering. No refund after 30 minutes or once the ordering window closes. Refunds are arranged directly between buyer and seller — FoodTime does not process payments.

Pickup & Delivery: FoodTime connects home cooks with nearby buyers only. It is not a delivery service — pickup or delivery must be arranged directly between buyer and seller.
